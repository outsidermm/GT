\textbf{Precedence of logical operations}

\medskip

\begin{tabular}{ll}
$()$ & 0 \\

\end{tabular}

\textbf{Logic puzzles}

\medskip

Angel tells the truth. Demon always lies.

\medskip

``Belial is an angel only when Melodi isn't an angel.''

\medskip

$b$ = Belial is an angel. $m$ = Melodi is an angel.

\medskip

\begin{tabular}{cc|c|c|c|c}
$b$ & $m$ & $\lnot m$ & $b \to \lnot m$ & $b \leftrightarrow (b \to \lnot m)$ & $m \land \lnot b$ \\
\hline
T & T & F & F & F & F \\
T & F & T & T & T & F \\
F & T & F & T & F & T \\
F & F & T & T & F & F
\end{tabular}

\medskip

$m \leftrightarrow (m \land \lnot b)$

\bigskip

3 chests. 1 treasure, 1 truthful chest.

\medskip

Chest 1: ``empty chest.'' \quad Chest 2: ``empty chest.'' \quad Chest 3: ``in chest 2.''

\medskip

$p$ = treasure is in chest 1 (chest 1's claim is $\lnot p$)

$q$ = treasure is in chest 2 (chest 2's claim is $\lnot q$)

$r$ = treasure is in chest 3 (chest 3's claim is $q$)

\medskip

\begin{tabular}{ccc}
$p$ & $q$ & $r$ \\
\hline
T & T & T \\
T & T & F \\
T & F & T \\
T & F & F \\
F & T & T \\
F & T & F \\
F & F & T \\
F & F & F
\end{tabular}

\medskip

Chest 2 is truthful; chest 1 has the treasure.
