\textbf{CS 2050 --- Notes 09/14}

\bigskip

\textbf{Theorem} --- a statement that can be shown to be true.

\medskip

Less important theorems are called propositions.

\medskip

Use proofs to show theorems are true.

\bigskip

\textbf{Proof} --- a valid argument that establishes the truth of a theorem.

\medskip

The statements used in a proof can be axioms (postulates) or other theorems.

\medskip

\textbf{Axiom} --- a statement we assume to be true.

\bigskip

\textbf{Lemma} --- a less important theorem used to prove complicated theorems.

\medskip

\textbf{Corollary} --- a theorem established directly from a theorem that has been proved.

\medskip

\textbf{Conjecture} --- a statement being proposed as true.
